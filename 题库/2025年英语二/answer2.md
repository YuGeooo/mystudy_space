## Section II Text 1

21. According to Paragraph 1, the practice of tipping in the U.S. ____[A]❌
A. was regarded as a sign of generosity.
B. was considered essential for waiters.
C. was a way of rewarding diligence.
D. was optional in most businesses.

22. Compared with tips in the past, today’s tips ____[C]✅
A. are paid much less frequently.
B. are less often requested in advance.
C. have less to do with service quality.
D. contribute less to workers’ income.

23. Tip requests are creeping into new kinds of services as a result of ____[A]✅
A. the advancement of technology.
B. the desire for income increase.
C. the diversification of business.
D. the emergence of tipflation.

24. The movement toward no-tipping services was intended to ____[C]❌
A. promote consumption.
B. enrich income sources.
C. maintain reasonable prices.
D. guarantee income fairness.

25. It can be learned from the last paragraph that tipping ____[A]✅
A. is becoming a burden for customers.
B. helps encourage quality service.
C. is vital to business development.
D. reflects the need to reduce prices.

---

### 批改结果

- 正确：3 / 5
- 得分：6 / 10（阅读 Text 1 每题 2 分）

### 订正

- **21. 正确答案 B（was considered essential for waiters）**：原文第一段说，美国顾客过去会给那些“主要收入来自小费”的人小费，例如工资低于最低工资标准的餐厅服务员。这说明小费对服务员来说是维持收入的重要/必要部分，故选 B。A 把 tipping 理解为“慷慨的标志”，原文未提；C 的 diligence（勤奋）在第一段没有依据；D 说大多数商家中小费是可有可无的，与原文相反。
- **24. 正确答案 D（guarantee income fairness）**：原文明确说 `To ensure that all employees were paid fair wages, some restaurants banned tipping and increased prices...`。禁止小费并提高菜价的目的是让所有员工获得公平工资，即保障收入公平。C “维持合理价格”与原文 `increased prices` 相矛盾。

## Section II Text 2

26. According to the first two paragraphs, the NHS ____[A]❌
A. is troubled by funding deficiencies.
B. can hardly satisfy people’s needs.
C. can barely retain its current employees.
D. is rivalled by private medical services.

27. One answer to the crisis in health and care is to ____[C]✅
A. boost the efficiency of hospitals.
B. lighten the burden on social care.
C. increase resources for primary care.
D. reduce the pressure on communities.

28. “Reimagining health” is aimed to ____[C]✅
A. reinforce hospital management.
B. readjust healthcare regulations.
C. restructure the health system.
D. resume suspended health reforms.

29. To maximise the nation’s health, the author suggests ____[D]❌
A. introducing relevant taxation policies.
B. paying due attention to social factors.
C. reevaluating major health outcomes.
D. enhancing the quality of healthcare.

30. It can be inferred that local leaders should ____[D]❌
A. exercise their power more reasonably.
B. develop a stronger sense of responsibility.
C. play a bigger role in the health system.
D. understand people’s health needs better.

---

### Text 2 批改结果

- 正确：2 / 5
- 得分：4 / 10（阅读 Text 2 每题 2 分）

### 订正

- **26. 正确答案 B（can hardly satisfy people’s needs）**：前两段的核心信息是 NHS 模式已经过时：等待名单超过 680 万，医疗对无力自费的人越来越难以获得，需求还在增长；这都说明 NHS 越来越难以满足人们的需求。A“funding deficiencies”虽然有“医疗服务成本挤压其他公共投资”作背景，但原文重点不是“NHS 自身资金不足”，而是服务可及性和需求满足问题。
- **29. 正确答案 B（paying due attention to social factors）**：原文说，医疗只解释了大约 20% 的健康结果，`Much more important are the places we live, work and socialise`，即居住、工作、社交等社会因素更重要，因此作者建议关注社会因素。D“enhancing the quality of healthcare”仍停留在“修 NHS”的医疗系统内部思路，与作者“rather than ‘fix’ the NHS”相反。
- **30. 正确答案 C（play a bigger role in the health system）**：最后一段提出要重新分配卫生系统中的权力和资源，并问哪些职能应下放给地方领导者。也就是说，地方领导者应在卫生系统中承担更大角色。D“understand people’s health needs better”有迷惑性，但原文说地方领导者 `with a much better understanding of the needs of their populations`，意思是他们本来已经更了解当地需求，不是在建议他们“应该更了解”。
